Executive Summary

Business Case & Scope

AON Network & CPM (Critical Path Method)

Hover over network nodes to view their project management definitions and task linkages. The Red Path indicates zero float.

The Critical Path (Zero Float)

The Red Path dictates the absolute minimum duration of the project. Activities like the STP Agile Sprints and UAT lie directly on this path. Because they possess zero float, a delay of even one day in any of these nodes will result in a day-for-day slip of the final Go-Live date.

35-Day Management Reserve

While the Steering Committee mandated a 180-day baseline, our AON network logic mathematically proves the core work can be completed in 145 days. This generates a massive 35-day Schedule Reserve, providing an excellent buffer against unforeseen regulatory or API integration blockers.

Parallel Execution Risk

The 1.4M Record DB Migration and ETL Pipeline (Tasks 4.1 & 4.2) are scheduled to run in parallel alongside the Agile Sprints. While they possess natural scheduling slack, data cleansing is historically high-risk. We strongly recommend executing them as early as possible rather than consuming their available float.

Schedule Crashing Scenario

Float Utilization (Leveling)

By resolving the dangerous double-booking of the Lead Cloud Architect, we had to delay their start on Data Cleansing. Fortunately, because that specific path possessed positive scheduling float, this resource leveling was achieved without delaying the final 145-day critical path.

Optimized Crash Cost

To meet the aggressive 130-day executive mandate, we applied Schedule Crashing specifically to the STP Engine. It was chosen because it sits directly on the Critical Path and offered the lowest crash cost-slope, allowing us to shave 15 days off the timeline for exactly ₹15 Lakhs.

Diminishing Returns Risk

We advise against further crashing. Injecting additional external contractors into complex Agile sprints invokes Brooks' Law (onboarding overhead outweighs velocity gains). Any further compression will likely result in exponential cost spikes and severe architectural quality degradation.

1. Purpose & Business Case

The legacy Core Banking System creates severe underwriting bottlenecks, requiring up to 48 hours for MSME and retail credit appraisal and contributing to a 32% application abandonment rate. The program will migrate to a cloud-native Core Banking Engine and implement automated Straight-Through Processing (STP) underwriting to support 4x loan-volume growth without operational disruption.

In Scope

  • STP underwriting engine
  • AA, CIBIL/Experian, NSDL/UIDAI and banking APIs
  • 1.4M-record extraction, cleansing and ETL
  • UAT, VAPT and production cutover
Constraints
  • RBI Digital Lending and DPDP compliance
  • Maximum 48-hour cutover
  • DBA migration specialists capped at 160 man-hours
  • Zero-loss migration expectation

4. Delivery Approach

Hybrid Agile-Waterfall. Waterfall governs initiation, regulatory compliance, architecture, database migration, integrated testing, cutover and closure. The STP engine is delivered through four two-week Agile sprints.
